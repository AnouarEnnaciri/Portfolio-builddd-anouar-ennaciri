Idempotency
Retrying a payment request must never move money twice.
Concurrency
Simultaneous requests against one account still have to leave the balance correct.
Recovery
A failure halfway through a transaction must not leave the books half written.
Reconciliation
Records from the payment flow are checked against a separate settlement source.
Testing
Integration tests run against a real PostgreSQL instance, and CI runs them on every push.
